# Memories soundtrack — licences

All tracks in this folder are **original works generated by code**, not
recordings or samples of anyone else's music. They are synthesised from scratch
by [`tools/gen_memory_music.py`](../../tools/gen_memory_music.py) (sine/saw
oscillators, noise percussion, a simple delay) and are dedicated to the public
domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>).
No attribution is required.

## Adding tracks

Only add music that is CC0 / public domain **and verifiable** (the licence is
stated by the author on the download page). Add a row above with the source URL,
author and licence, then list the file in `kTracks` in `src/MemoryManager.cpp`.
Do not add CC-BY, "royalty-free" (Pixabay, YouTube Audio Library, …) or
unverified tracks.
